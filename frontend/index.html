THE CHALLENGE

Forgery is a security risk

!

Problem Statement

Fake and tampered identity documents create security risks in government services, banking, employment, education, and other verification processes.

⌁

Why It Matters

Manual inspection is time-consuming and may not reliably detect sophisticated alterations or manipulated document images.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

AI-assisted document screening

Users upload an identity document and receive an automated first-level assessment from a trained deep-learning image model. The system presents a verification score, confidence, genuine probability, and fake probability to support further manual or official verification.

ABOUT

AI Based Document Screening

The system accepts an identity document image, sends it to the verification API, and displays the AI prediction and probability values. It is designed as a preliminary screening tool, not a replacement for official document verification.
